# INC-1017: connection leak during peak sale

Background tasks in user-api failed to close database sessions. PostgreSQL reached its maximum connection limit and requests queued. The incident was resolved by rolling back the worker change and restarting affected workers. The similar symptom does not prove the same service caused a later incident.
